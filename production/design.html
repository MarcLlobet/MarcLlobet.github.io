Type

Space Grotesk for everything you read, JetBrains Mono for counters, languages and links. Sizes are fluid: every step is a clamp of the viewport, so a short screen gets smaller headings, not a scrollbar.

The line

Nothing changes colour on hover. A 2px line wipes in from the start of the word and leaves through its end; where a project owns the element, the line takes its accent.

Mockups

Each project is drawn, not screenshotted: an SVG page built at the exact pixel size of its box. Change the shape of the box and the columns, rows and lines are counted again to fill it. Point at one to see the loop it plays.

One project, one screen

A case is a counter, the name, the frame and the text, and it is at least as tall as the viewport: the frame takes whatever the words leave. This is the real component, boxed so you can see where it ends.

The impossible triangle

The mark is a Penrose triangle drawn in WebGL. On the home page it flies from the hero to the counter, through every project and into the footer, taking the accent of the one it is passing. Here it docks in the slot below: the same renderer, the same model.

Three beams fly in and lock on load and the pointer tilts them. At prefers-reduced-motion the model is still the model: built, still, and never swapped for a flat drawing.
